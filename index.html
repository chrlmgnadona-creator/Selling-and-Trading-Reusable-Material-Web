LGU Tacloban Environmental Services

Material Exchange & Reuse Guidelines

Ensuring safe, legal, and community-friendly resource recovery across Tacloban City.

Offline Agreements & Safety First

This platform does not process online payments or shipping. All trades and purchases are arranged directly between parties. We recommend meeting in well-lit public spaces or designated barangay collection points.

Accepted Reusable Material Categories

Plastics & Glass

PET bottles, plastic crates, clean jars, and glass containers. Must be rinsed and free of hazardous residues.

Metals & Construction

Scrap iron, aluminum cans, corrugated GI sheets, wooden pallets, and surplus bricks.

Textiles & Apparel

Clean cloth scraps, bulk fabrics, and reusable garments suitable for upcycling initiatives.

Electronics & Appliances

Non-working appliances for parts recovery, e-waste components, and cables.

Prohibited Items

  • Hazardous medical waste, infectious materials, and used syringes.
  • Single-use contaminated plastics soaked in toxic chemicals.
  • Asbestos or lead-painted construction debris.
  • Unregistered or stolen goods.
